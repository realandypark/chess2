How to play

  • A compact chess game on a 3×4 board
  • Tap a piece, then tap a square to move it
  • The dots show where each piece can go

The pieces

    Goal

    • Capture the king: take your opponent’s king to win
    • Reach the back rank: you also win if your king reaches your opponent’s back rank and cannot be captured on the next move

    Special rules

    • Promotion: a pawn that reaches your opponent’s back rank becomes a queen
    • Deploying captured pieces
      • Every piece you capture goes to your reserve tray
      • Tap a reserve piece, then tap any empty square to deploy it
      • Deploying uses your turn, so choose between moving and deploying
      • A queen you capture goes to your reserve as a pawn
    • Draw: if the same position occurs three times, the game ends in a draw
